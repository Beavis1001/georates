“Booking.com pays”: what is behind the online-payment discount?

Data 21 Sep–22 Nov 2026 (GeoRates log), as of 23 Nov 2026

Some offers on Booking.com are cheaper if you pay online straight away, instead of at the hotel. GeoRates recognises that note in the offer and shows it in the result as “Online payment discount”. Here we explain what is behind it, how often we have seen it, and what it has to do with country prices.

Pay online or pay at the hotel

On Booking.com there are two ways to pay:

  • At the hotel: you book, and you pay the hotel directly on arrival or departure.
  • Online through Booking.com: Booking handles the payment (“Payments by Booking.com”) and passes the money on to the hotel.

According to Booking.com for Partners, guests who pay online cancel less often than guests who pay at the hotel. For hotels, that is a reason to reward online payment with a lower price. How large the discount is, and whether there is one, is in the offer. Booking does not state a fixed size in the sources we checked.

What our data shows

Since 21 Sep 2026 GeoRates stores the discount labels Booking shows in the offer. In 140 checks with a result (21 Sep–22 Nov 2026):

  • An online-payment discount was labelled in the offer 65 times. That made it the most common label, ahead of “Genius possible” (24), Genius (10) and early booker (9).
  • In the 65 checks with an online-payment discount, GeoRates found a country at least 1% cheaper 42 times (median 2.9%). In the 75 checks without that label it was 30 times (median 0.7%).

That does not mean the discount causes the country price. We store the label per check, not per country. We therefore do not know whether the discount was the same size in all 15 country sessions. It only shows that offers with an online-payment discount and country differences often appeared together in our data.

What we do not measure: the price of the same room when you pay at the hotel. We do not know the gap between “pay online” and “pay at the hotel”.

Online-payment discount and country price

A country price and an online-payment discount do not rule each other out in our data. The point that matters: if you pay online through another country, the charge is often in a foreign currency. The way you pay then decides how much of the advantage is left.

The EU Geo-blocking Regulation does not allow different prices by origin inside the EEA. A country inside the EU therefore usually brings nothing. Only countries outside the EEA are worth considering.

How to go about it

  1. Check your hotel with GeoRates. If the result says “Online payment discount”, the price is for paying online.
  2. In the booking step, choose the same payment method the offer names. Otherwise the price can be higher.
  3. Compare the final price including taxes and fees for the same room, with the same board and the same cancellation terms.
  4. Pay in the property's currency, not in Booking's euro conversion, and use a card with no foreign-currency fee.

Caveats

  • Paying online often means paying earlier. Read the cancellation terms. A non-refundable rate is not a fair comparison with a flexible one.
  • Foreign currency: if Booking charges in another currency, bank fees and the conversion rate can cost part of the discount.
  • Prices move: a discount can be gone again after a few minutes. Better to check twice, then book promptly.

Frequently asked questions

What does “Booking.com pays” mean?

Payment runs online through Booking.com instead of directly at the hotel. Some hotels give a lower price for that.

How large is the online-payment discount?

The hotel sets it. We did not find a fixed size in the Booking sources. We only measure whether the label is in the offer.

How often is there a discount?

In 65 of 140 GeoRates checks (21 Sep–22 Nov 2026) an online-payment discount was labelled.

Can I combine the discount with a country price?

In our data the two often appeared together. We do not measure whether the discount is the same size in every country. Always compare the final price.

Do I need a VPN for this?

No. The online-payment discount also exists from Germany. You only need a VPN if another country is cheaper.
