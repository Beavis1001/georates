Is a VPN worth it inside the EU? The Geo-blocking Regulation explained

Data as of 7 November 2026

A popular tip goes: “Just book your hotel in Spain through a Spanish VPN server and it will be cheaper.” That sounds logical, but inside the EU it usually does not work. Here is why, and what our measurements show.

What the Geo-blocking Regulation covers

The EU Geo-blocking Regulation forbids traders from offering customers in the EU different prices or conditions because of their nationality, place of residence or location. That expressly includes hotel stays (Verbraucherzentrale, EUR-Lex). A hotel in Mallorca may not, as a rule, charge you more from Germany than it charges someone from Spain.

That does not apply to countries outside the EU. Hotels can set discounts on Booking.com for particular countries or regions (Booking.com for Partners), for example for guests from the United States or India.

What our data shows

Alongside the 15 fixed countries (all of them outside the EU except Germany), GeoRates sometimes also asks for the country the hotel is in. Since 19 Sep 2026 that has given us prices from EU countries such as Spain, Italy, Portugal, Greece and Croatia in 37 checks, 42 EU prices in all.

  • 33 of 42 EU prices were within ±1% of the German price, and most of them matched to the cent.
  • 3 were cheaper, 6 were more expensive. In 5 of those 9 gaps the same price also appeared in one of the German control fetches. The price was already moving inside Germany.
  • For comparison: in 26 of 87 checks with several German fetches, the German price itself already moved by at least 1%.
  • It was similar for users in Austria and France: in 6 of 8 cases their home-country price matched the German one, and the 2 gaps also showed up in German fetches.

In those same 37 checks, a country outside the EU was at least 1% cheaper 21 times, and 10% or more 8 times. Most often: the United States (12), India (6), Colombia (2), Peru (1).

Across all hotels in EU countries since 19 Sep (47 checks with a result), another country was at least 1% cheaper in 28 cases, almost always the United States (21) or India (6). The median of those hits: 4.65%.

A Spanish or Italian VPN server does next to nothing in our data. Countries outside the EU are the interesting ones, including for hotels in Europe.

Caveats

  • A moving price is not a country price: search twice in a row and you sometimes see different prices, even without a VPN. Compare only checks from the same run.
  • Genius and mobile rates: these discounts are tied to your account or device, not to the country, and they distort the comparison.
  • Switzerland and the United Kingdom are not in the EU. We have little or no measurement data for them, so we make no claim here.
  • Actually getting a non-EU price: if you pay in a foreign currency, you need a card with no foreign-currency fee. Do not book “residents only” rates.

How to go about it

  1. Skip trying EU servers one by one.
  2. Check your hotel with GeoRates. It compares 14 countries outside the EU with the German price, in the same run and including taxes.
  3. If a non-EU country is at least a few percent cheaper, connect a VPN to that country and book there.
  4. Check the room, board, cancellation terms and final price before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does a VPN with an EU server produce cheaper hotel prices?

Almost never in our data. 33 of 42 EU prices were within ±1% of the German price, and the gaps were mostly explained by ordinary price movement.

Why are prices the same inside the EU?

The EU Geo-blocking Regulation forbids different prices or conditions by nationality or place of residence inside the EU, including for hotels.

Can a hotel in Spain be cheaper from the United States?

Yes. The regulation applies only inside the EU. In our data, hotels in EU countries were cheapest most often from the United States.

Which countries are worth it instead?

In our data, mainly the United States and India. It depends on the hotel, though, so the check is worth doing for each booking.

Is booking with a VPN legal?

Using a VPN is legal in Germany. You should not book rates that are expressly only for residents.
