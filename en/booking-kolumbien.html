Booking from Colombia on Booking.com: how much can you actually save?

Data 19 Sep–15 Nov 2026 (GeoRates log), revised 16 Nov 2026

Colombia is often named in forums as a tip for cheaper Booking.com prices. In an early forum sample of ours (July to September 2026, before our Genius correction on 18 Sep) many double-digit savings did in fact come from Colombia, up to 29.4%. Those figures are not in our log today. Our newer, cleaner data shows a much more sober picture.

What our data shows

From the GeoRates log (178 checks with a result, 19 Sep to 15 Nov 2026):

  • Colombia was the cheapest of 15 countries 21 times. 15 of those had an advantage of at least 1%, and only 3 times was it 10% or more (14.5%, 14.7% and 17.1%).
  • When Colombia won by at least 1%, the saving had a median of 1.5%.
  • Only a single Colombia hit was confirmed as stable on a second fetch: a hotel in the United States, €366.84 → €313.82 (14.5% / €53.02, 5 Nov 2026). For comparison: 28 finds of at least 1% were confirmed as stable in this window, 10 of them from the United States and 10 from India.
  • Direct comparison of Colombia against Germany (162 checks with both prices): Colombia was at least 1% cheaper in 33 cases (about 20%), practically the same in 58 cases (±1%), and at least 1% more expensive in 71 cases. Median: −0.9%, so usually a little more expensive.
  • In 19 of the 33 cases where Colombia was cheaper than Germany, another country was cheaper still.
  • Largest gap between Colombia and Germany: 49.9% (a hotel in Thailand, €400.01 → €200.26, 20 Sep 2026). Argentina was cheaper still there, at 51.1%.

Conclusion: Colombia can be worth it, but in our data it is the exception rather than the rule. Check your actual hotel instead of switching blindly to a Colombian server.

Why Colombia is sometimes cheaper

We do not know for certain. A plausible explanation: hotels can set discounts for particular countries and regions on Booking.com (Booking.com for Partners). Rounding when pesos are converted into euros adds to that. An advantage of 0.5% is therefore not a real country price. We only measure what is displayed.

Caveats

  • Not every hit holds: two of the three large Colombia hits (14.7% and 17.1%) had changed on the second fetch and were marked “not stable”.
  • Genius: if you book while logged in and have Genius level 2 or 3, you may already see a better price in Germany. Compare the final price at checkout then.
  • EU rule: inside the EU, switching country usually does nothing because of the Geo-blocking Regulation. Colombia is outside it, which is why country prices are possible there at all.
  • Residents only: do not book rates that expressly apply only to residents of Colombia.

How to book a Colombia price

  1. Check your hotel with GeoRates. The rest is worth it only if Colombia is shown clearly cheaper (a few percent, not 0.5%).
  2. Connect a VPN to a server in Colombia and open Booking.com in a private window.
  3. Check the room, board, cancellation terms and the final price including taxes.
  4. Pay in pesos (COP) with a card that has no foreign-currency fee, and decline Booking’s conversion into euros.

Worked example (from our log)

A hotel in the United States, checked on 5 Nov 2026: €366.84 from Germany, €313.82 from Colombia, so €53.02 (14.5%) less. The hit was stable on a second fetch. Take off VPN costs (if you do not already have a subscription) and any card fees.

Frequently asked questions

Is Booking.com always cheaper from Colombia?

No. In our data since 19 Sep 2026, Colombia was at least 1% cheaper than Germany in only about 20% of cases, and more expensive in 71 of 162 cases.

How much can I save from Colombia?

When Colombia was the cheapest country, the median was 1.5%. Only 3 of 21 Colombia hits were 10% or more.

Are there better countries than Colombia?

In our data the United States and India were cheaper much more often, and were also confirmed as stable more often. It still depends on the hotel.

Why were the Colombia figures higher in the forum?

Those figures come from an early sample from before our method correction on 18 Sep 2026. Newer measurements show large savings less often.

Is booking with a Colombian VPN legal?

Using a VPN is legal in Germany. You should not book rates that expressly apply only to residents of Colombia.
