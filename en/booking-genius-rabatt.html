Genius on Booking.com: levels, discounts, and why Genius still shows when you are logged out

Data 21 Sep–15 Nov 2026 (GeoRates log), as of 16 Nov 2026

Genius is Booking.com’s free loyalty programme. For comparing prices between countries it is one of the biggest sources of error. We learned that ourselves: on 18 Sep 2026 we corrected our measurement because Genius displays had distorted the results. Since then our guides use only data from 19 Sep 2026 onward.

The three Genius levels

According to Booking.com:

  • Level 1: 10% off selected stays. A free account is enough for that.
  • Level 2: 10–15% off, plus breakfast or an upgrade at some properties. From 5 bookings in 2 years.
  • Level 3: 10–20% off, from 15 bookings in 2 years.

Important: the discount applies only at participating properties, it is taken off the price before taxes and fees, and you have to be logged in. A higher level does not automatically mean a larger discount: each property sets the percentage itself.

Why you still see “Genius” when you are logged out

Booking often shows a Genius badge, or a note about a possible Genius discount, to visitors who are not signed in. The discount is only taken off when you book while logged in. The price on screen and the price at checkout can therefore differ.

In our data (115 checks with a result from 21 Sep to 15 Nov 2026, since we started recording discount notes):

  • In 32 checks Booking showed Genius in at least one country: 10 times as “Genius”, 22 times as the note “Genius possible”.
  • Genius is not the same in every country: at a hotel on Rhodes (24 Sep 2026) the Genius discount appeared in the US session and not in the German one. The final price there was 9.6% lower.

What that means for comparing countries

GeoRates measures while logged out, all countries in the same run. That is fair for comparing countries, but it says nothing about your personal Genius price. That is why the tool lets you optionally enter the price you are seeing yourself.

What came out of that (30 checks with a price the user entered, 21 Sep–15 Nov 2026):

  • In 12 cases the price the person was seeing was at least 1% below our logged-out price, between 4.3% and 23.2%.
  • In 11 of those 12 cases their own price was cheaper than every country we measured.
  • In 13 cases it was practically the same, and in 5 cases higher.

We cannot see which discount is behind it. Account discounts such as Genius are the likely explanation. For you that means: check while logged in first, then compare countries.

Booking says country prices can be combined with Genius discounts (Booking.com for Partners). Whether that happens at your hotel only shows up at checkout.

How to go about it

  1. Sign in to Booking.com and note the final price including taxes for your room.
  2. Check the hotel with GeoRates and enter your price in the field “Price you currently see”.
  3. If a country outside the EU is clearly cheaper, compare there while logged in as well, through to checkout. Only the final price counts.
  4. For large amounts, comparing checkout in two countries is worth it, because Genius is not taken off the same way everywhere.

Caveats

  • Genius changes with the account: only you can see your level and the discounts, while logged in. Our figures are logged-out prices.
  • EU rule: inside the EU, prices must not differ by where you come from. Genius is an account discount and sits outside that rule.
  • Taxes: Genius applies before taxes. With fixed fees per night, the saving on the final price is smaller than the percentage.

Frequently asked questions

What does Genius do on Booking.com?

According to Booking, level 1 is 10% off at participating properties, level 2 is 10–15% and level 3 is 10–20%. The discount applies only while logged in, and on the price before taxes.

How do I reach Genius level 2 or 3?

Level 2 with 5 bookings in 2 years, level 3 with 15 bookings in 2 years. Level 1 comes with a free account.

Why does Booking show “Genius” when I am not logged in?

Booking advertises the possible discount even when you are logged out. It is only taken off when you book while logged in. Always check the final price at checkout.

Is Genius the same in every country?

Not necessarily. At a hotel on Rhodes, our data had the Genius discount in the US session and not in the German one.

Is my Genius price cheaper than a country price?

Sometimes. In 11 of 30 checks where someone entered their own price, that price was cheaper than every country we measured. Compare while logged in first.
