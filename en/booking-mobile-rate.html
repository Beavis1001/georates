Mobile rate on Booking.com: is the app really cheaper?

Data 24 Sep–15 Nov 2026 (GeoRates log), as of 16 Nov 2026

“Book in the app, it’s cheaper there.” You hear that tip often. Booking.com does offer what it calls mobile rates. We measured how often the smartphone was actually cheaper in practice.

What a mobile rate is

According to Booking.com for Partners:

  • A mobile rate is a discount that only people using the Booking app or a mobile browser can see.
  • The discount must be at least 10%.
  • The hotel decides whether the rate applies in the app and the mobile browser or in the app only.
  • Mobile rates can be combined with Genius, but not with prices for specific countries.

Whether a hotel offers a mobile rate at all is up to each hotel.

What our data shows

Since 24 Sep 2026, GeoRates has also fetched the price with a phone profile on many checks (mobile browser, not the app), from the same country as the computer price. Result from 57 checks (24 Sep–15 Nov 2026):

  • 40 times the smartphone price was practically the same as on the computer (±1%).
  • 5 times the smartphone was at least 1% cheaper: 3.4%, 5.2%, 7.0%, 10.0% and 10.1%. Four of those were confirmed on a second fetch.
  • 12 times the smartphone was more expensive. In 6 of those cases the same price also turned up on one of our computer fetches. The price was moving between sessions, not because of the device. We always compare against the lowest computer price.
  • Only once did the smartphone beat all 15 countries: €138 on the computer, €124 on the smartphone (10.1%, confirmed, 6 Nov 2026). In 3 of the 5 cheaper cases a country price was cheaper still.

For context: according to Booking, a mobile rate has to be at least 10% off. The advantages of 3.4%, 5.2% and 7.0% are below that. They are probably not a real mobile rate, but ordinary fluctuation between two sessions – the same logic as the 0.2% on the Egypt page.

Conclusion: mobile rates exist, but they were rare in our measurements. Only 10.0% and 10.1% reach Booking’s threshold of at least 10%.

What we do not measure: mobile rates the hotel has switched on for the app only. We cannot query the app itself. The app can therefore have discounts that are missing from our figures.

Mobile rate or country price?

Booking says mobile rates cannot be combined with country prices. A VPN country price plus a mobile discount on top is therefore not to be expected. We have not measured the two together. In practice that means: compare both routes and take the cheaper one.

The EU Geo-blocking Regulation does not matter here: the mobile rate is tied to the device, not the country. It therefore also applies to bookings from Germany.

How to go about it

  1. Check your hotel with GeoRates. If the result has a smartphone row, it sits directly under the baseline country.
  2. Open the hotel link on your phone in the browser or in the Booking app, and compare the final price for the same room with the same board and cancellation terms.
  3. If a country outside the EU is cheaper than the smartphone, book via that country. Otherwise book on the phone, with no VPN at all.
  4. If you have a Booking account, check while logged in as well: Genius can apply on top of the mobile rate.

Caveats

  • Not every hotel has a mobile rate, and some apply in the app only.
  • Prices move: a difference between phone and computer can also be an ordinary swing between two fetches. Better to check twice.
  • Compare like with like: room, board, cancellation terms and the final price including taxes have to match.

Frequently asked questions

Is Booking.com cheaper in the app?

Sometimes. In 57 measurements with a mobile browser the smartphone was at least 1% cheaper 5 times, and the same price 40 times. We could not measure app-only rates.

How large is a mobile rate on Booking.com?

According to Booking, at least 10%; the hotel sets the exact size. In our data the confirmed smartphone advantages were between 3.4% and 10.1%. Values below 10% are probably not a real mobile rate, but ordinary fluctuation between two sessions.

Can I combine a mobile rate and a country price?

Not according to Booking. Mobile rates can be combined with Genius, but not with prices for specific countries.

Do I need a VPN for the mobile rate?

No. The mobile rate is tied to the device, not the country. You only need a VPN for a country price.
