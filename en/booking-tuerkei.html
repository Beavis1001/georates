Booking from Turkey on Booking.com: what we know and what we don’t

Data as of 7 November 2026

Turkey shows up in plenty of VPN guides as a country where hotels are supposed to be cheaper. Before you go looking for a Turkish VPN server, here is an honest frame: Turkey is not one of the 15 countries GeoRates measures from. We therefore cannot say with any confidence whether Booking.com is cheaper from Turkey. What we can show is how prices for hotels in Turkey behaved in our measurements.

What our data shows (and what it doesn’t)

  • GeoRates compares the same room, in the same run, from 15 country sessions: Germany, Colombia, Argentina, Egypt, India, Vietnam, Indonesia, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Peru, Mexico, the Philippines, Thailand, the United States and Japan. Turkey is not among them.
  • On two checks for hotels in Turkey, GeoRates also tried a Turkish session. Both times Turkey returned no price. There is therefore no measured “Turkey price” in our log.
  • For hotels in Turkey, the GeoRates log (16 Sep–7 Nov 2026) has 10 checks with a result, covering 5 hotels. Only 2 of them are from after our Genius correction on 18 Sep.

The two more recent measurements:

  • All-inclusive resort, double room, not free to cancel (21 Sep 2026): €968.09 from Germany, €929.67 from India, so €38.42 (4.0%) less. Japan was almost level, at 3.8% less, and Argentina was 7.5% more expensive. This check was not fetched a second time.
  • All-inclusive suite (5 Nov 2026, baseline country Australia): €3,470.01 against €3,343.29 from India, so €126.72 (3.7%) less. A second fetch confirmed the figure. From Thailand, the United States and Japan the same suite was about 7.7% more expensive than from Germany.

Earlier measurements (16–18 Sep 2026, before the Genius correction, only partly informative): hotels in Istanbul, at Istanbul airport and in Antalya-Konyaaltı. Depending on the room, India, Japan, the United States or Indonesia came out ahead, by 0.3% to 13.3%. At one Istanbul hotel the cheapest country changed from room to room.

The sample is small. It does show that hotels in Turkey can have country prices, and that India was ahead in both of our more recent cases. Whether it would be cheaper still from Turkey itself, we do not know.

Why country prices are possible for hotels in Turkey

Hotels can set discounts on Booking.com for particular countries or regions (Booking.com for Partners). Turkey is not in the EU. The EU Geo-blocking Regulation, which forbids price differences by place of residence inside the EU (Verbraucherzentrale), does not apply to prices shown to visitors from Turkey. Country prices are therefore possible in principle. How often, and how large, we cannot say without our own Turkey measurement.

Caveats

  • No Turkey data of our own: claims such as “always X% cheaper from Turkey” we can neither confirm nor refute.
  • Check that the terms match: all-inclusive, room category and cancellation terms have to be identical. Otherwise you are comparing different offers.
  • Resident-only rates: if a rate says it is only for residents or nationals of a country, you should not book it. That can cause trouble at check-in.
  • Paying in lira: if you pay in Turkish lira (TRY), use a card with no foreign-currency fee and decline Booking’s conversion into euros.

How to go about it

  1. Check your hotel with GeoRates first. In one run you see whether one of the 15 measured countries (India, for example) is cheaper.
  2. If you also want to test Turkey, connect a VPN to a Turkish server and open Booking.com in a private window.
  3. Compare exactly the same room, with the same board and the same cancellation terms, and always the final price including taxes and fees.
  4. Book the country with the lowest final price, and pay in local currency with a card that has no foreign-currency fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is Booking.com cheaper from Turkey?

We cannot say. Turkey is not one of the 15 countries GeoRates measures, and two extra attempts from Turkey returned no price.

Does GeoRates measure prices from Turkey?

No. GeoRates compares Germany, Colombia, Argentina, Egypt, India, Vietnam, Indonesia, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Peru, Mexico, the Philippines, Thailand, the United States and Japan.

Are hotels in Turkey cheaper from other countries?

Sometimes. In our two more recent measurements a hotel in Turkey was 4.0% and 3.7% cheaper from India than from Germany and Australia respectively. The sample is small, though.

Does the EU geo-blocking rule apply to Turkey?

No. Turkey is not an EU member. The regulation does not apply to prices shown to visitors from Turkey.

Which currency should I pay in?

In local currency, with a card that has no foreign-currency fee. Booking’s conversion into euros is usually more expensive.

Is it legal?

Using a VPN is legal in Germany. You should not book rates that are expressly only for residents.
